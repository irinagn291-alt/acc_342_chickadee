Chickadee is a private evening ritual for writing down the day and winding down before bed. Move through Reflect, Breathe, and Ambient, then seal the night on your device.

• Write a “Release line” in each chamber or skip it.
• Follow the timed Breathe count with “Settle,” “Draw,” “Release,” and “Rest” cues.
• Use “Advance” to move through the chambers and “Seal night” to save the ritual.
• Track your streak, “Nights,” and “Best” totals.
• Browse sealed nights in newest-first “History” and open read-only night details.
• Adjust chamber durations and the “Haptics” setting in “Settings.”
• Continue an unfinished ritual when you reopen the app.
• Keep ritual lines, settings, and sealed nights on this device.

For a quiet end-of-day writing ritual, with no system permissions requested.
